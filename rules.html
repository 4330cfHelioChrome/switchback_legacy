Game Rules

1. Overview

A fast-paced, competitive shedding card game for 2–6 players. Players take turns playing cards in ascending or descending order based on the central pile. The first player to empty their hand wins the game.

2. Components & Setup

  • Deck: Numbered cards ranging from 80 to 200 (configured by the host).
  • Players: 2 to 6 players.
  • Initial State: Cards are dealt evenly based on player count. One card is flipped to form the Center Pile (Midpoint determines Ascending or Descending start).

3. Gameplay

On your turn, play a card matching the active direction:

  • Ascending: Play a card strictly higher than the Center Pile.
  • Descending: Play a card strictly lower than the Center Pile.

If you cannot or choose not to play, you must either Draw 1 Card or Change (reverse the direction and skip your turn).

4. Winning the Game

The first player to successfully play or discard all cards in their hand wins immediately.
